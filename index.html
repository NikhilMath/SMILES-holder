Why I built this

I built this website to turn any molecule into a 3D-printable pen holder. 🧪

Paste in a molecule’s SMILES string (the one-line text code chemists use to describe a structure), and the site creates a 3D printer file of that molecule’s skeletal formula, built as a pen holder you can keep on your desk.

I started with one molecule that means a lot to me: , which my dad synthesized during his career as a chemist. Seeing something he made in the lab turn into an object I can hold and put on my desk was a great moment.

That’s when I realized this would make a great gift for:

  • 🔬A chemist who’d like their favorite molecule (or their own work) on their desk
  • 🎓A chemistry grad or student finishing a big milestone
  • ☕Anyone who’d like a pen holder shaped like , or

To help you get started, I’ve put together a library of ready-made molecules. You can also enter any SMILES you like.
